## How to read the portfolio

The relationship between the works is a research direction, not a chain of proofs that transfers results automatically. UCP states terms for an exchange; its implementation and evaluation are documented in this repository. Formal results, exploratory model behavior, implemented controls and philosophical commitments answer different questions. The [research appendix](RESEARCH.md) records reviewed limits, while the [experiment directory](../experiments/README.md) contains UCP-specific evidence.

Each linked work retains its own licensing terms. This directory neither republishes their full texts nor changes those terms.
