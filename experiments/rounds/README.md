# Three rounds of GPT-4o review and revision

English is primary · [Русская версия](README.ru.md) · [Main invitation](../../README.md)

This bounded development study continues from the same 24 original proposals in the previous GPT-4o run. All three conditions start from identical drafts. Over three rounds, ordinary second look (S), an actual observer (O), and observer plus computed diagnostics (K) reconsider their own latest proposals. Actor and reviewer conversations preserve their prior literal messages. The full case facts appear once; later turns identify the current candidate by content hash.

[Prospective plan](PLAN.md) · [Results](RESULTS.md) · [Operational review-loop profile](../../docs/ITERATIVE_REVIEW.md) · [Evidence archive](../data/rounds-08.zip) · [Checksums](../data/SHA256SUMS.txt)

The cases, rules, reference optimum and guard are unchanged. A shared instruction now emphasizes checking the actual grounds of a review, preserving useful permitted actions and considering further feasible value. This is a specific iterative workflow on known tasks, not a pure test of additional turns or a new holdout. The previous full-optimum result remains unchanged. This study prospectively asks an admissibility/utility question and keeps optimal joint success visible as a secondary result.

There are 36 logical API completions: 12 review batches, 18 plan batches and 6 final-report batches. Each batch contains 12 cases. All participants use GPT-4o through OpenRouter with OpenAI-only routing. No tools, computed optimum or root-agent correction are supplied. Only K actors receive deterministic checks of their current proposal; their reviewers do not receive those checks.

Intermediate plans are dry-run proposals. They do not execute, consume case resources or create simulated effects. After round 3, each final plan executes once through the atomic guard, then its author receives a real simulator receipt and reports in the same conversation. These are synthetic effects, not operations in an external application.

## Interpretation

All rounds are reported, including unchanged proposals, lost utility, new errors and reversions. The headline uses round 3, not whichever round looks best. Invalid reviews remain invalid and use a declared ordinary-checklist fallback. Exact quotations, correct verdicts and supported reasons are separate checks.

The fixed semantic subset contains 24 reviews: four known difficult cases in repeat 2, O and K, all three rounds. It is targeted and nonblind, not representative of all 144 reviews. Arithmetic assesses all reviews and plans. Sessions and templates are clustered; the results do not establish general reliability or an intrinsic property of consciousness.
